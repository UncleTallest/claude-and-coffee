# Claude and Coffee Austin

**Building continuity with Claude through documentation and community.**

Welcome to the resource hub for Claude and Coffee Austin - a meetup for people working with Claude who want to move beyond one-off conversations and build systems that remember, learn, and improve over time.

## What This Is

A collection of practical guides, real-world examples, and community wisdom for creating continuity with Claude. Whether you're technical or not, these resources will help you build systems that work the way you think.

## What You'll Find Here

### [Getting Started](getting-started/why-guides.md)

The fundamentals - why you need structured documentation, and what happens when you don't have it.

### [Core Concepts](core-concepts/)

Communication Guides, Session Summaries, and the layers that create continuity: Personal Preferences, Project Instructions, Project Files, and external storage.

### [Intermediate Techniques](intermediate/)

The refinements that make systems actually work - positive framing, explicit naming, smart context loading, and regular auditing.

### [Implementation](implementation/)

Practical setup guides for Google Drive-based systems, session workflows, and starter templates you can copy.

### [Case Studies](case-studies/)

Real systems built by real people. Learn from what actually works in practice.

### [Resources](resources/)

Meetup schedule, useful links, and community connections.

## Who This Is For

- **Non-technical users** who want Claude to remember context without fighting it every session
- **Developers** building AI-powered workflows and need persistent state
- **Anyone frustrated** with re-explaining themselves to Claude constantly

## The Philosophy

These systems work because they externalize continuity. Instead of hoping Claude remembers, you build documents that travel with you. The architecture does the heavy lifting so you can focus on actual work.
